# Privacy policy draft — Bible Maps

**Product:** Bible Maps Chrome extension (v1.0.0)  
**Effective:** 2026-11-02  
**Contact:** nst@meritssllc.com
**Public URL:** https://www.meritssllc.com/bible-maps-pp/

## Summary

Bible Maps helps you see biblical places and journeys while reading Scripture. Detection runs **locally in your browser** with a curated gazetteer included in the extension. The extension **does not** require an account and **does not** send your verse text to an AI or remote geocoding API for matching.

## Data the extension accesses

- **Page content on supported Bible sites** (hosts declared in the manifest) solely to find curated place/journey names, highlight them, and show them in the side panel.
- **Optional remote map tiles** from Esri ArcGIS Online public canvas services when you are online and view areas outside the bundled offline map extract.
- **Optional navigation** to Google Maps when you choose a modern-maps / directions link.

## Data we do not collect

- No Bible Maps user accounts or cloud sync in v1.0.0
- No sale of personal data
- No AI processing of Scripture text for place detection

## Permissions

Permissions are limited to side panel UI, light preference storage, tab/side-panel coordination, and the host permissions required for Bible-site content scripts and optional map/link providers. See the Chrome Web Store listing for the current permission list.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the store listing as required.
